We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you play our game on the TikTok platform.

📌 1. Information We Collect

As a TikTok Mini Game, we primarily access information through TikTok’s APIs:

  • TikTok OpenID/UnionID: A unique identifier provided by TikTok to recognize you.
  • Profile Information: Your nickname and avatar (only if you grant explicit permission).
  • Gameplay Data: Level progress, high scores, and in-game currency balances.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and language settings to ensure technical compatibility.

⚙️ 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for:

  • Providing and maintaining the Game’s core functionality.
  • Saving your game progress and synchronization.
  • Displaying leaderboards within the TikTok ecosystem.
  • Improving game performance and fixing technical bugs.

🤝 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. However, we share data with:

  • TikTok (ByteDance Ltd.): To facilitate game integration and analytics.
  • Service Providers: Cloud storage or analytics services that comply with international data protection standards (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

🇺🇸 4. Regional Compliance (US Users)

In compliance with TikTok’s "Project Texas" requirements, any US user data processed by this Game is handled in accordance with TikTok’s designated secure infrastructure and data residency policies.

📋 5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to your data, request deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"), or opt-out of data collection. To exercise these rights, please contact us via the email below.

🧸 6. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.
